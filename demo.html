Step 1 — Build the Segment Navigate to Segments → Create. Add rule: "Last Order more than 60 days ago" AND "Lifetime Value greater than $50". Click Preview — you'll see 18 matching guests. Save as "Lapsed High-Value Guests".
Step 2 — Create the Coupon Navigate to Coupons → Create. Code: COMEBACK20. Type: Percentage. Value: 20%. Expiry: 14 days. Save the coupon — note the unique code.
Step 3 — Create the Campaign Navigate to Campaigns → Create. Choose template "Win-Back". Attach segment "Lapsed High-Value Guests". Insert COMEBACK20 coupon into email body. Set subject: "We miss you — here's 20% off". Schedule for tomorrow 10 AM.
Step 4 — Sync to Google Ads Navigate to Integrations → Google Ads. Select the "Lapsed High-Value Guests" segment. Click Sync — CDP hashes emails with SHA-256 and uploads the audience to Google Customer Match. Set up a retargeting campaign in Google Ads targeting this audience.
Step 5 — Measure Results After 7 days, navigate to Campaigns → COMEBACK20 Win-Back → Reports. View delivery funnel, open rate (31%), click rate, coupon redemptions, and 7-day revenue attribution. The platform matches redeemed coupons back to the original segment.

Step 1 — Identify New Guests Navigate to Segments. You'll see a pre-built segment "New This Week" — guests whose first order was in the last 7 days. This segment auto-updates daily via Hangfire.
Step 2 — Build the Welcome Journey Navigate to Journeys → Create. Trigger: "New customer joins segment New This Week". Add steps: (1) Send Welcome Email immediately, (2) Wait 3 days, (3) Check: has guest ordered again? (4a) Yes → End. (4b) No → Send "Come back, here's a reward" email with WELCOME14 coupon.
Step 3 — Set Up Birthday Automation Add a secondary journey: Trigger "Birthday approaching (7 days out)". Send a birthday reward email with a unique coupon code. Watch Journeys execution logs to see Hangfire firing each step in real time.
Step 4 — Monitor the Journey Navigate to Journeys → Welcome Journey → Details. See how many guests are at each step, how many have completed, and the revenue attributed to journey-triggered orders over 7 days.

Stop Paying for Regulars
Exclude your best guests from paid ads — save budget, improve ROAS
+ROAS
4.2×
ROAS

Wasted Spend
Step 1 — Identify Your Regulars Navigate to Segments and find the pre-built "Champions" segment — guests with RFM score ≥ 12/15 who order at least twice a month. These guests don't need incentivized ads; they already come back.
Step 2 — Create a Suppression Audience in Google Ads Navigate to Integrations → Google Ads → Sync Audience. Select the "Champions" segment. Toggle to "Exclusion list". CDP hashes all emails, uploads to Google Customer Match as a suppression list. Your ad campaigns now skip these guests entirely.
Step 3 — Repeat for Meta Ads Navigate to Integrations → Meta Ads. Same process — upload Champions as a custom audience suppression list on Meta. Both suppression lists update automatically on a daily schedule.
Step 4 — Watch ROAS Improve Navigate to Analytics → Reports. Compare ad spend efficiency before and after suppression. With regulars excluded, your budget focuses entirely on acquisition and lapsed re-engagement — the guests who actually need an incentive. Typical result: 40% reduction in wasted ad spend.
Consent Note Every ad sync checks the guest's consent status. Guests who have opted out of marketing are automatically excluded from all audience uploads regardless of segment membership. Navigate to Guests → Any guest → Privacy tab to see consent flags in action.
