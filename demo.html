Step 1 — Build the Segment

Segments → Create. Add rule: "Last Order more than 60 days ago" AND "Total Orders ≥ 2". Click Preview — see the 18 matching guests. Save as "Lapsed High-Value Guests".

Step 2 — Create the COMEBACK20 Coupon

Coupons → Create. Code: COMEBACK20. Type: 20% off. Min order: $15. Max uses per guest: 1. Expiry: 14 days. Save and copy the code.

Step 3 — Create the Win-Back Campaign

Campaigns → Create. Choose "We Miss You" template. Attach "Lapsed High-Value Guests" segment. Insert COMEBACK20 in the email. Subject: "We miss you at Acme Burger 🍔 — here's 20% off". Schedule for 10 AM tomorrow.

Step 4 — Sync to Google Ads

Integrations → Google Ads → Sync. Select the segment. CDP hashes emails with SHA-256 and pushes to Google Customer Match as a retargeting audience — reaching the same guests on Google Search and YouTube.

Step 5 — Measure Results

After 7 days: Campaigns → Win-Back → Reports. See delivery funnel, 31% open rate, coupon redemptions, and revenue attributed to the campaign within the 7-day post-send window.

Journeys → "Welcome Series". See the 3-step flow: (1) Thank-you email with WELCOME14 (15% off) immediately, (2) Wait 3 days, (3) Condition: has guest ordered again? If no → send reminder email. If yes → end journey.

Step 1 — View Active Regulars Segment

Segments → "Active Regulars (Last 30 Days)". This contains 48 guests with LifecycleStage = Active. These guests already come back — spending ad budget to reach them wastes money.

Step 2 — Check the Google Ads Exclusion

Integrations → Google Ads → "Exclude Active Regulars". See the sync status: 48 guests sent, 37 matched (82% match rate), 3 excluded due to no advertising consent. Refreshes every Monday automatically.

Step 3 — See the Consent Gate

In the sync run history, notice "Consent Excluded: 3". These 3 guests have advertising_consent = opted_out. CDP removes them from the upload automatically — no manual checking needed. Every sync is GDPR-safe by default.

Step 4 — Understand the Business Impact

With 48 regulars excluded from your Google new-guest campaign, your ad budget focuses entirely on prospects and lapsed guests. Industry benchmark: 2–4× better ROAS. Typical 40% reduction in wasted ad spend.
